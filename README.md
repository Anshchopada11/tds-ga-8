## 📊 Overview

This project analyzes the **customer retention rate** for an e-commerce company over four quarters in 2024. The analysis compares the company's performance against the **industry benchmark target of 85%**, which is critical for sustaining long-term growth.

---

## 📈 Data Summary

| Quarter | Retention Rate (%) |
|---------|---------------------|
| Q1      | 66.04               |
| Q2      | 67.01               |
| Q3      | 76.38               |
| Q4      | 78.20               |

- **Average Retention Rate (2024)**: **71.91**
- **Industry Target**: **85.00**

---

## 🧠 Key Findings

- Retention rates improved significantly over the year, increasing from **66.04% in Q1 to 78.20% in Q4**.
- Despite the growth, the **average retention rate of 71.91%** still falls short of the **industry benchmark of 85%**.
- The most substantial jump was observed between Q2 and Q3, suggesting positive impact from mid-year initiatives.

---

## 📉 Business Implications

- **Revenue Risk**: Falling short of industry benchmarks could lead to lost repeat business and reduced customer lifetime value (CLTV).
- **Increased Acquisition Costs**: Low retention puts pressure on marketing to acquire new users constantly.
- **Operational Inefficiencies**: Without loyal customers, fulfillment, support, and personalization processes become more expensive.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

To bridge the 13-point gap and hit the industry target of 85%, the company should:

1. **Implement Targeted Retention Campaigns**:
   - Personalized email marketing
   - Loyalty programs and tiered rewards
   - Post-purchase engagement automation

2. **Improve Onboarding Experience**:
   - Ensure customers see value quickly (faster first purchase or engagement)

3. **Leverage Customer Feedback**:
   - Run NPS surveys and follow up with detractors

4. **Predict Churn Early**:
   - Use machine learning to identify at-risk customers and act preemptively
